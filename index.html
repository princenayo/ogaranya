Our Services

We offer comprehensive legal services across multiple practice areas to meet all your legal needs.

Corporate & Commercial Law

We handle company registration, filing annual returns, ensuring proper corporate governance, and maintaining your company's records with the official registry (the CAC) to keep your business in good legal standing.

Litigation & Dispute Resolution

We represent our clients in cases involving debt recovery, breach of contract, landlord/tenant issues, professional negligence, and other conflicts where a legal solution is sought.

Real Estate & Property Law

Land Acquisition, Title Perfection, Property Disputes, Leases. We guide you through all aspects of property transactions and disputes.

Criminal Defense

Expert representation for various criminal charges with a focus on protecting your rights and achieving the best possible outcome.

Charity Law

We assist with incorporation, applying for tax-exempt status, ensuring compliance with regulatory bodies, and advising on governance matters specific to non-governmental Organizations.

HR and Employment Advisory

We guide businesses in drafting employment contracts, creating company policies, handling disciplinary issues, and ensuring compliance with Nigerian labour laws.
